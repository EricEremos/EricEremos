## I take ideas through to the product people see.

I'm **Eric (Hyunjun Kim)**, studying **Marketing & Information Systems at HKUST**. I bring marketing judgment into the work of making software: understand the need, shape the product, build it, check it, and give people a clear reason to care.

For my independent products, I work across **research → planning → design → engineering → release → marketing**. I also contribute to university language-learning projects, including **XiYouQuest, a winning team project at the HKUST GenAI Hackathon 2026**.

The work below starts with everyday questions: how do you keep a place, start a conversation, return to a thought, or understand what is happening on your Mac? Each showcase connects the idea to the product, with real screens and a clear account of my role.

### How I carry the work through

I research the question before deciding what to make. Then I review the brief, make the direction visible, build a first version and check it against the original purpose. Release preparation, the product story and feedback belong to that same cycle.

In my current workflow, **ChatGPT supports research, planning and building; Claude helps refine the design details and finish the work**. I set the direction and quality criteria, inspect the results and keep the decisions worth reusing. The aim is to make something useful and explain it as carefully as it was built.

The showcases identify current captures, design previews, sample data and availability. Team credits stay with the team; private implementation and personal records stay private.

Away from the screen, I sing in a band.
