### Selected work

Three examples of how I connect a product's purpose, the experience of using it and the story that introduces it.

### Say-On · Every choice has a story.

A conversation card game for the moment after a choice. Open a room, turn over a question and discover how differently everyone sees it.

**My role:** Independent concept, product planning, interface design, development and product presentation.  
**Try it:** Playable in your browser.

### MELI · Bring the course into view.

**My role:** Independent product direction, design, engineering and showcase development.  
**Status:** In development; explore the current product through its showcase.
