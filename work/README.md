# The work, and my part in it

I study Marketing & Information Systems at HKUST. Across team projects and apps of my own, I am interested in how a product's purpose carries through its workflow, interface and explanation.

The public showcases below let you see the products without access to private source repositories. Captions distinguish current screens, earlier captures and sample content.

## With the team

### MELI

**A clearer next step for every language class.**

Since March 2026, I have worked on language-learning software with HKUST's Center for Language Education. My contributions include learning workflows, UI/UX, backend integration and testing, assessment materials, data analysis and university SSO integration. MELI is a team effort; the public page describes my contribution without presenting the whole product as individual work.

[Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/meli-showcase) · [Team organisation](https://github.com/Meliedu)

### XiYouQuest

**Mandarin practice with a journey to follow.**

XiYouQuest brings Putonghua practice into a Journey to the West learning experience. My contributions span planning, learning flows, interface, backend and regression tests.

One concrete example is the speaking exercise: learners can replay the reference, record themselves and listen back. The change prevents recording during reference playback, releases the previous recording and adds regression tests around the flow.

[Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/xiyouquest-showcase) · [Team source](https://github.com/baduru11/XiYouQuest-RPG-study-web) · [Speaking-flow contribution](https://github.com/baduru11/XiYouQuest-RPG-study-web/commit/2469cefd609cba3154a87a52138dc824160a5ea4)

## Personal projects

### Say-On

**Every choice has a story.**

A browser card game designed to get people talking. People join the same room, make a choice and reveal it together, leaving space for the conversation that follows. The public page includes the game flow, animated demonstrations and the story behind the name 사연.

[Play Say-On](https://say-on.vercel.app/) · [Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/say-on-showcase)

### KOWLO

**A Hong Kong worth remembering.**

A personal photo atlas built around districts, places and the memories attached to them. Release-142 work on 22 October 2026 refines the station-inspired opening, photo pins and walking experience. The showcase pairs those release notes with honestly dated development screens; the older atlas images contain illustrative places and photos.

[Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/kowlo-showcase)

### Yeoback

**Make room with a clearer view of what is there.**

A native Mac storage workbench focused on the decision before removal. File paths, sizes, selection and review belong together, so clearing space begins with understanding the files. The public light and dark screenshots use test fixtures.

[Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/yeoback-showcase)

### Qav

**Find where your connection gets tangled.**

A Mac connection diagnostic tool that follows the route through Wi-Fi, router, provider, VPN and the internet. Its working interface separates status, possible fixes and results, helping make a connection problem easier to investigate.

[Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/qav-showcase)

### REED

**Keep what was said.**

A voice notebook in development for Mac and iPhone. Recording and readable transcripts help make a spoken thought easier to return to. The public iPhone example uses sample content; device sync is planned rather than presented as a finished feature.

[Product showcase](https://github.com/EricEremos/reed-showcase)

## What these projects have in common

I research the problem, prepare a brief, make the intended result visible, build and test it, then retain the decisions that help the next iteration. My current AI workflow uses ChatGPT for research, planning and building, with Claude for design detail and finishing. I judge the result through the actual interface and behaviour, then revise it.
